# Video Project Brief: Claude Video Editing Workflow

## Overview
- **Project Name**: Claude Video Editing Workflow
- **Format**: 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920)
- **Target Platforms**: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, X (Twitter)
- **Target Runtime**: ~58 seconds (8 Beats)
- **Primary Style**: Split-Screen Tech Explainer (Brand Kit compliant)

## Core Narrative Arc
Show how an AI coding assistant (Claude) orchestrates a professional video editing pipeline using FFmpeg, Whisper, Remotion, and HyperFrames without manual timeline dragging.

## Pacing & Editing Guidelines
1. **Hook**: First 2.5 seconds must show immediate high-energy visual split and bold title.
2. **Visual Rhythm**: Cut or visual element trigger every 1.8 – 2.5 seconds.
3. **Dead Air**: All pauses > 250ms trimmed out.
4. **Color Accent**: Electric Blue (`#38BDF8`) for tech/Claude, Neon Green (`#22C55E`) for automation/success, Attention Yellow (`#FACC15`) for caption highlights.
5. **Captions**: Dynamic centered pill captions with word-by-word emphasis.

## Beat Breakdown
- **Beat 01 [0:00 - 0:04.2]**: Hook — "I turned Claude into my full-time video editor..."
- **Beat 02 [0:04.2 - 0:11.0]**: Architecture — Studio Folder Structure (`References`, `Brand Kit`, `Raw`, `Exports`)
- **Beat 03 [0:11.0 - 0:18.0]**: Style Ingestion — Frame extraction every second via FFmpeg
- **Beat 04 [0:18.0 - 0:25.5]**: Synthesis — Writing `Brand Kit/brand-kit.md`
- **Beat 05 [0:25.5 - 0:33.7]**: Cutting A-Roll — Whisper speech recognition & silence stripping
- **Beat 06 [0:33.7 - 0:42.7]**: Motion Graphics — Programmatic animations with Remotion & HyperFrames
- **Beat 07 [0:42.7 - 0:51.5]**: Review & Approval — Interactive Storyboard Dashboard
- **Beat 08 [0:51.5 - 0:57.5]**: Outro & CTA — Final render & export pipeline
