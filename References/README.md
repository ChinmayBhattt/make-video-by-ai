# References

Videos whose style you like and want your edits to feel similar to.

## What goes here
- Video files (or a `links.txt` with URLs) from creators or brands you admire
- Optional: a short note per video on what you like (pacing, captions, transitions, color, music, hooks)

## Tips
- Name files clearly, e.g. `creator-name_fast-cuts-captions.mp4`
- 5-10 strong references beat 50 random ones
- These are for inspiration only. Do not put your own finished work here (use `Approved Videos`).
