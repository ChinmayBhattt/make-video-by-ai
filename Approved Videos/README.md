# Approved Videos

Your own finished edits that you're happy with. This is the "this is exactly my style" folder.

## What goes here
- Final videos you've published or signed off on
- Optional: a short note per video (what worked, platform, performance)

## Tips
- Only add edits you'd be happy to have copied again
- Name files with a date, e.g. `2026-11-02_product-launch.mp4`
- Move videos here from `Exports` once approved
