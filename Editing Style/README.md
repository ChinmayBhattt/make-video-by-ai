# Editing Style

Written rules describing how your videos should be edited. Think of it as your style guide.

## What goes here
- `style-guide.md`: pacing, cut length, caption style, transitions, zooms, sound effects, color grading, hook structure
- Do's and don'ts (e.g. "no cheesy transitions", "always cut silences")
- Platform notes: aspect ratio, length targets for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube
- Anything learned from feedback on past edits

## Tips
- Keep it short and specific. "Cut every pause over 0.4s" beats "make it punchy".
- Update it whenever you give feedback on an edit so it carries over to the next one.
