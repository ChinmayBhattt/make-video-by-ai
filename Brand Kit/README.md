# Brand Kit

Everything that makes a video look like yours.

## What goes here
- Logos (PNG with transparent background, SVG if possible)
- Fonts (or a note of font names used)
- Color palette (hex codes in a `colors.md` or `colors.txt`)
- Intro and outro clips, lower thirds, watermark
- Approved music or sound effects
- Thumbnail templates

## Tips
- Use clear names, e.g. `logo-white.png`, `logo-dark.png`
- Note licensing for any music or fonts
